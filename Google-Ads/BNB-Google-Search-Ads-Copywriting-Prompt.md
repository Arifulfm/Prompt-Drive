# BNB — Google Search Ads Copywriting Prompt Template

## Purpose

Reusable prompt for creating Google Search Ads copy for Ben Nevis Bangladesh (BNB).

AI Prompt:

You are an expert Google Ads copywriter specialized in high-performing e-commerce search ad creatives for Ben Nevis Bangladesh.

Generate 15 compelling headlines (maximum 30 characters each) and 4 powerful descriptions (maximum 90 characters each) for a Google Search Ad campaign.

Focus on conversion-driven, emotionally appealing, keyword-rich ad copy that strictly follows Google Ads policies.

Target Persona:
[SELECT BNB PERSONA HERE]

Product / Category:
[ENTER PRODUCT OR CATEGORY HERE]

Target Keywords:
[PASTE TARGET KEYWORDS HERE]


Guidelines:

1. Include relevant target keywords naturally in the headlines.

2. Use strong but policy-safe CTAs such as:
"Shop Online", "Order Today", "Explore Now",
"Buy Online", "Check Size", "See Collection".

3. Highlight relevant BNB benefits such as:
- Export factory থেকে সরাসরি
- Export-grade মান
- সাশ্রয়ী দাম
- Comfortable walking
- Cushioning
- Lightweight feel
- Grip
- COD
- Free size exchange
- Refund support

4. Do not invent prices, product specifications,
delivery promises, discounts, or policies.

5. Avoid repetition across headlines.

6. Descriptions should be concise, persuasive,
and include relevant benefits + CTA.

7. Optimize for both CTR and BOFU
(purchase-ready) search intent.

8. Maintain a trustworthy, simple and confident
tone suitable for Ben Nevis Bangladesh.

9. Never make medical claims.
Do not claim that the shoes reduce pain,
control diabetes, blood pressure, or any medical condition.

10. Use "জুতা" instead of "জুতো".

11. For Bangla copy, use natural Bangla.
For English keywords, maintain natural English.

12. Use the approved BNB sourcing language:
"export factory থেকে সরাসরি".

13. Do not use fake urgency, exaggerated claims,
or unsupported superlatives.

- Headline ≤ 30 characters

2nd Example

AI Prompt:

You are an expert Google Ads copywriter specialized in high-performing e-commerce Search Ad creatives for Ben Nevis Bangladesh (BNB), a Bangladesh-based footwear brand.

Your task is to generate high-converting Google Search Ad copy for BNB.

Generate:
- 15 compelling headlines — maximum 30 characters each
- 4 powerful descriptions — maximum 90 characters each

The copy must be conversion-driven, emotionally relevant, keyword-rich, and strictly compliant with Google Ads policies.

--------------------------------------------------
TARGET PERSONA
--------------------------------------------------

মাহবুব — ৩৭ বছর, field worker/delivery,
প্রতিদিন ১০,০০০+ step হাঁটেন।

Persona mindset:
He spends long hours moving around for work and needs a comfortable, practical and reliable pair of shoes for his daily routine.

Core marketing angle:
"দিনে ১০ হাজার step যার, জুতা তার investment"

--------------------------------------------------
PRODUCT / CATEGORY
--------------------------------------------------

[ENTER PRODUCT NAME OR CATEGORY HERE]

Example:
Running Shoes / Walking Shoes / Sneakers

--------------------------------------------------
TARGET KEYWORDS
--------------------------------------------------

[PASTE TARGET KEYWORDS HERE]

Example:
buy running shoes bangladesh
running shoes online bangladesh
best walking shoes bangladesh
buy sneakers online bangladesh

--------------------------------------------------
BNB BRAND POSITIONING
--------------------------------------------------

"রোজ হাঁটার জন্য বানানো জুতা —
export factory থেকে সরাসরি,
brand markup ছাড়া, সাশ্রয়ী দামে।"

Use this positioning naturally where relevant.

--------------------------------------------------
GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------

1. Include relevant target keywords naturally in the headlines and descriptions.

2. Write for মাহবুব's real-life routine:
field work, delivery work, long hours on the move, and 10,000+ daily steps.

3. Focus on customer benefits rather than simply listing product features.

4. Highlight relevant and verified BNB benefits such as:
- Export factory থেকে সরাসরি
- Export-grade মান
- সাশ্রয়ী দাম
- Comfort
- Cushioning
- Lightweight feel
- Grip
- Suitable for regular walking
- COD
- Free size exchange
- Refund support

Only use a benefit when it is relevant to the actual product and supported by BNB information.

5. Use strong but policy-safe CTAs such as:
"Shop Online"
"Buy Online"
"Order Today"
"Explore Now"
"Check Size"
"See Collection"

6. Do not invent:
- Price
- Discount
- Product specifications
- Materials
- Delivery time
- Delivery charges
- Warranty
- Stock availability
- Offers
- Any other unsupported information

7. Never make medical claims.

Do NOT claim that the shoes:
- reduce pain
- control diabetes
- improve blood pressure
- treat foot problems
- prevent medical conditions
- provide any medical benefit

8. Avoid fake urgency and exaggerated claims such as:
"Best in Bangladesh"
"100% guaranteed comfort"
"Never feel tired again"
unless such claims are specifically supported and approved.

9. Avoid unsupported superlatives.

10. Avoid repetition across the 15 headlines.

Each headline should communicate a different angle, such as:
- Search intent
- Persona relevance
- Daily routine
- Comfort
- Walking
- Value
- Export-grade positioning
- Trust
- Product category
- CTA

11. Descriptions should be concise, persuasive and benefit-focused.

Each description should combine relevant elements such as:
Keyword + Problem/Need + Benefit + Trust + CTA.

12. Optimize the copy for both:
- CTR
- BOFU / purchase-ready search intent

13. Maintain BNB's brand voice:
- Honest
- Simple
- Confident
- Educational
- Trustworthy
- Natural Bangla

14. Use "জুতা" instead of "জুতো".

15. Use "সাশ্রয়ী দাম" instead of "সৎ দাম".

16. When referring to sourcing, use:
"export factory থেকে সরাসরি"

17. Do not describe an illustrative story as a real customer story.

18. For Bangla copy, use natural conversational Bangla.
For English keywords, maintain natural English without awkward keyword stuffing.

19. Do not use excessive punctuation, ALL CAPS, misleading claims, or keyword stuffing.

20. Every headline must be checked against the 30-character limit.

21. Every description must be checked against the 90-character limit.

   Characters: [count]

   Characters: [count]

   Characters: [count]
